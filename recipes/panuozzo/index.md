It's possible to make a bunch of dough, bake them in the oven, and freeze them.

The ingredients below are classic, but any thing that can go in a sandwich is good too.

## Ingredients
- Pizza dough
- Mozzarella
- Eggs
- Pesto
- Some leavey vegetable
- Some charcuterie
- Optional: quick-pickeled vegetable

### Steps
- Prepare oven at max temperature with a pizza steel
- Stretch pizza dough into a bagette-like shape
- Prepare the ingredientes that wil go in the oven separated from the ones that will be added cold
- Place the pizza dough in the oven
- Open the panuozzo once it is not too warm
- Add all the filling that will be in the oven
- Once the ingredients are done take the whole thing out of the oven
- Add the cold ingredients
- Fold and cut in half

> If warming up a frozen dough, the oven does not need a pizza steel but just a hot broiler
