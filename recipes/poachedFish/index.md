# Poached Fish

## Ingredients
- Oil
- Onion
- Fish, cod is nice

## Steps
- Place a lot of oil in a skillet that can go into the oven.
- Warm up the oil gently to reach 80°C.
- Preheat the oven to 120°C.
- Cut and peel one or two onions into half and peel.
- Place the onion in.
- Place the fish in.
- Add a lid that is also oven proof.
- Place the skillet into the oven for 30 minutes.
- Remove from oven.
- Add plate to oven to warm up.
- Plate fish.
- Finish frying the onions.
- Plate the onions.
- Use the oil for a sauce.
