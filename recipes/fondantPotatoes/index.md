## Ingredients
- Potatoes
- Beef stock
- Butter
- Neutral oil
- Onion
- Garlic
- Thyme

## Steps
- Cut each potato into two flat sides to create a flat shape
- Warm up a skillet that can go into an oven into medium to medium-high heat with some neutral oil
- Add the potatoes
- Preheat the oven to 180 degrees
- Flip them occasionally until each has two brown sides
- Chop onion
- Chop garlic
- When potatoes are fully browned, add onion
- Add garlic
- Add stock
- Add thyme
- When it is boiling, place a heat resistant lid and place in the oven for 20 minutes
- Flip them and check the doneness
- Wait a few minutes and repeat. They should take around 35 minutes.
- When the potatoes are done, take them out of the oven, plate the potatoes.
- For the sauce, either:
  - Pass the liquid throught a sieve and reduce the sauce
  - Just reduce and serve with the garlic and onion
  - Blend everything adding some butter while blending
