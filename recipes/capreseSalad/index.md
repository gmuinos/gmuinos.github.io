# Caprese Salad

## Ingredients
- Tomatoes
- Mozzarella
- Fresh basil leaves
- Olive oil
- Balsamic vinegar
- Salt, flaky is better.
- Pepper

## Steps
- Slice the tomatoes
- Slice the mozzarella
- Break the basil leaves with hands
- Mix them either stacking or just placing them irregularly.
- Add olive oil on top
- Add balsamic
- Add salt and pepper
