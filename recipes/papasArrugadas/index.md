# Papas Arrugadas

## Ingredients:
- Small potatoes
- Salt, around 250gr per kilo of potatoes
- Water

## Steps:
- Wash the potatoes well.
- Add the potatoes to a large pot
- Barely cover the potatoes in cold water
- Add a lot of salt
- Heat the pot until boiling
- Wait around 15 minutes, checking for doneness sometimes
- Once ready, separate the potatoes from the water with a sieve
- Return the potatoes to the pot
- On low to medium heat, warm the potatoes up until they are completely dry on the outside, shaking the pot sometimes
