# Mojo Rojo

## Ingredients
- Garlic
- Red bell pepper
- Red chilly pepper
- Cumin
- Paprica
- Olive oil
- Neutral vinegar
- Salt

## Steps
- To a food processor, add the garlic and blitz.
- Pre-chop the bell pepper, add to the food processor and blitz
- Add the chilly pepper, cumin, paprica, and salt and blitz
- Add oil, vinegar and salt without blitzing
- Mix with a spoon and check for acidity and salt
