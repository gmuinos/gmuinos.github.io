# Mojo Verde

## Ingredients
- Parsley and/or cilantro
- Garlic
- Green bell pepper
- Cumin
- Olive oil
- Vinegar
- Salt

## Steps
- To a food processor, add the garlic and blitz.
- Add the parsley/cilantro and blitz.
- Pre-chop the bell pepper
- Add the bell pepper to the food processor and blitz
- Add the cumin and salt and blitz
- Add oil and vinegar without blitzing
- Mix with a spoon and check for acidity and salt

# Mojo Rojo

## Ingredients
- Garlic
- Red bell pepper
- Red chilly pepper
- Cumin
- Paprica
- Olive oil
- Neutral vinegar
- Salt

## Steps
- To a food processor, add the garlic and blitz.
- Pre-chop the bell pepper
- Add the bell pepper to the food processor and blitz
- Add the chilly pepper, cumin, paprica, and salt and blitz
- Add oil and vinegar without blitzing
- Mix with a spoon and check for acidity and salt

# Tips
- It's possible to make green and reuse the utensils for the red, but not the other way around. 
- It's raw garlic, so use only a bit.
- Use less cumin with the green mojo than the red.
